# Getting Started

## The layout
Every page in Jot has the same layout:

- **Sidebar (left):** the Jot logo and two groups.
  - **Overview:** Dashboard *(Planned)*, Tasks, Activity
  - **Settings:** General *(Planned)*, Roles
  - Click the collapse button at the top of the sidebar to shrink it to icons, and click it again to expand it.
- **Header (top):** the workspace name on the left and your **user menu** on the right.
- **Content (center):** the page you're on.

## User menu
Click your avatar or name in the top-right corner to open it. It shows your name and email, plus:
- **Profile:** your personal details
- **Preferences:** app settings such as the theme
- **Switch workspace:** move to another workspace you belong to
- **Sign out**

Click anywhere outside the menu to close it.

## Your first five minutes
1. Open **Tasks** and type a task title in the box at the top, then press **Enter**.
2. Click the task to open its details and add a description.
3. Tick the checkbox when the task is finished.
4. Owners and Admins: open **Settings → Roles** to check who can do what.
5. Open **Activity** to see your changes logged.

## Light and dark
Jot uses the dark theme by default and also has a light theme.
