# FAQ

**I can't see Activity in the sidebar.**
Your role doesn't have `activity:read`. Ask an Owner or Admin to add it.

**Why can't I edit the Owner role?**
It's locked so a workspace can never lose full control.

**What happens to people when I delete their role?**
They stay in the workspace without a role until someone gives them a new one.

**I unticked a Done task and it went to To do, not In progress. Why?**
The checkbox only switches between Done and To do. Use the status buttons in the task details to pick In progress.

**Can I undo deleting a task?**
No, deleted tasks can't be restored.

**Can I add my own fields to tasks?**
Not yet. v1 has title, status and description. Custom fields are *Planned*.
