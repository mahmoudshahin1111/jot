# Tasks

The Tasks page holds all the work in your workspace. The summary under the title shows how many tasks the workspace has and how many are done.

## Add a task
Type a title in **Add a task and press Enter**, then press **Enter** or click **Add task**.
- New tasks start as **To do** with an empty description.
- A blank title is ignored.

## Statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| To do | Not started |
| In progress | Someone is working on it |
| Done | Finished |

## List view (default)
- Tasks are grouped by status in the order To do, In progress, Done. Each group header shows the status and a count.
- Click the arrow on a group to collapse or expand it.
- Each row shows a checkbox, the title and the description. The status isn't repeated on each row because the group header already shows it.
- **Checkbox:** tick it to mark a task **Done**. Untick it to send the task back to **To do**.
- Done tasks show a struck-through title.

## Board view
Use the **List / Board** switch to see the tasks as cards in three columns, one per status.
- Click a card to open its details.
- To move a card to another column, change its status in the details panel. *(Drag and drop is Planned.)*

## Filter and search
- **Status chips** (All, To do, In progress, Done) show one status at a time. The number on each chip is the total for that status.
- **Search** looks in titles and descriptions as you type.
- The filter and the search work together, in both views.

## Task details
Click any row or card to open the panel on the right, where you can:
- Edit the **title** and **description**
- Change the **status** with the three buttons
- **Delete task**. Deleted tasks can't be restored.

Close the panel with the **X**.

## Who can do what
Viewing and changing tasks depend on your role's permissions. See [Permissions Reference](Permissions-Reference.md).
